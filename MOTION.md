# Motion asset production

The app uses local Animated WebP images, not embedded video players. Each dynamic asset runs at 24 fps with a three-second loop. The source is AI-created key poses, interpolated with FFmpeg optical-flow motion estimation. These are illustrative animations, not live-action recordings.

Style prompt: natural photorealistic male trainer, navy athletic T-shirt, charcoal shorts and black trainers; normal athletic build, white seamless studio; no exposed anatomical muscle overlays. Same person and camera across chronological 4-by-3 pose sheets.

Romanian deadlift: upright with bar at thighs, progressively hinge hips back with neutral spine, lower bar close to legs to mid-shin, then return upright.

+## pushup

a standard floor pushup viewed from side three-quarter view. Straight line shoulders hips ankles, feet and palms remain fixed. Frame1 straight-arm high plank, frames2-6 progressively bending elbows lowering chest toward floor, frame7 chest just above floor elbows approximately90degrees, frames8-12 push body up together to straight-arm plank. No knee pushups.

## row

barbell bent-over row in a fixed hip-hinged stance torso about35degrees above horizontal, knees softly bent, neutral spine. THREE-QUARTER SIDE VIEW. Frame1 arms straight hanging bar below shoulders, frames2-6 pull bar toward lower ribs while elbows pass torso, frame7 bar at waist, frames8-12 extend arms to lower bar. Torso and feet NEVER move, only elbow/shoulder motion.

## lunge

bodyweight REVERSE lunge, no weights, hands resting on hips. Three-quarter side view. Frame1 stand tall feet together, frames2-4 step right foot backward progressively, frames5-7 lower back knee near floor while front left foot fixed and knee bent about90degrees, frames8-10 straighten and drive through front leg, frames11-12 return rear foot forward and stand. Whole body full view every cell.

## goblet

kettlebell goblet squat with ONE black kettlebell held securely by horns at upper chest throughout. Three-quarter front view. Frame1 standing feet shoulder width, frames2-6 gradually lower into squat with knees tracking toes and heels down, frame7 bottom squat thighs parallel floor, frames8-12 rise to standing. Both hands stay at chest and kettlebell same position relative chest. Feet fixed.

## press

standing strict barbell overhead press, front three-quarter view. Frame1 bar resting in front of upper chest elbows below bar, frames2-6 press bar gradually up close to face then overhead, frame7 arms fully extended overhead bar over midfoot, frames8-12 lower back to upper chest. Standing tall entire time, no squat no leg drive, feet fixed, no leaning back. Bar must be overhead in frame7, all bar and body within every cell.

## kbdeadlift

two-handed kettlebell deadlift viewed from side three-quarter. ONE kettlebell held by handle in both straight arms between legs. Frame1 stand tall with bell at groin, frames2-6 gradually hinge hips back and bend knees to lower kettlebell, frame7 kettlebell contacts floor BETWEEN feet with flat neutral back and bent knees, frames8-12 rise to standing. No swing. Feet fixed.

## deadbug

bodyweight dead bug exercise, side three-quarter floor view entire person. Supine on back, head shoulders and low back stay on mat. Frame1 both arms vertical to ceiling, hips and knees90degrees (tabletop), frames2-6 extend LEFT arm overhead toward floor behind head and RIGHT leg almost straight low above floor simultaneously while opposite limbs remain tabletop; frame7 fully extended opposite arm and leg, frames8-12 return to tabletop. Only two arms and two legs, don't switch sides midway. Natural perspective and accurate opposite arm/leg coordination.

## bridge

BARBELL GLUTE BRIDGE ON THE FLOOR. NOT hip thrust on a bench. Exact side view person supine on mat, shoulders and head on floor throughout, both knees bent feet flat fixed. Barbell across HIP CREASE/pelvis secured by both hands; NOT stomach or knees. Frame1 hips resting floor, frames2-6 progressively lift pelvis, frame7 hips lifted until shoulder hip knee align straight diagonal with shins nearly vertical, frames8-12 lower pelvis to floor. Head shoulders feet and barbell remain in correct locations every frame. No bench, no chair.

## shouldertap

high plank alternating shoulder tap. Front three-quarter view person supported by straight arms and toes, feet hip width apart. Frame1 both palms on ground, frames2-4 raise RIGHT hand across chest to touch LEFT shoulder, frame5-6 return right palm ground, frames7-10 LEFT hand raises touches RIGHT shoulder, frames11-12 return palm to ground. Stable hips neutral straight torso, supporting arm always straight, no pushup, all limbs anatomically correct.

## Holds

Forearm plank: elbows below shoulders, forearms on floor, straight trunk and legs. Side plank: right elbow under shoulder, right forearm supporting, legs straight and stacked, left hand on hip. The opposite side mirrors this image. Squat hold uses the comfortable bottom position of the goblet squat asset. These exercises intentionally use still hold poses.

Review: the dead-bug sequence uses the first six poses and returns through those poses, excluding a generated frame with an incomplete leg. Shoulder taps are reordered to keep each supporting arm planted until the tapping hand returns. All assets are newly generated, with no third-party media embedded.
